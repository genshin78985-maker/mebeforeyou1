Lead-in DiscussionPre-reading

Discuss the questions in pairs. Write short notes next to each question — you will need them for the class discussion.
1. What makes a perfect holiday for you: active sports, sightseeing, food, relaxation?
2. Is it easy or difficult to travel with a person who has a disability? Why?
3. What qualities does a person need to work as a carer / companion?
4. Look at the title Me Before You. What do you think the film is about?
5. Have you ever had to change your plans because someone you love needed help?

8

DiscussionPost-reading

Discuss in groups. Write your ideas in the boxes.
1. Why does Louisa stay with Will? Is it only because of money?
2. Do you think Will is cruel or just deeply unhappy? Give examples.
3. What does “live as fully as possible” mean for Will? For Louisa?
4. Should Louisa try to change Will’s mind? Why / why not?
5. How does the film show the difference between pity and care?
6. Is it possible to be a “friend” when you are paid to care for someone?
